Clean everywhere: no horizontal overflow (0/54), all tables have matching th/td counts, no broken images, body bg #f7f8fa, Heebo, primary buttons 44/r14, CLS ≤ 0.02 on every screen.

## P0 — visible breakage
1. **All 17 manager desktop screens: the sidebar nav runs under the user block.** The «הודעות» group (מסרונים, מייל, התראות) is hidden behind «רונית כהן / מנהלת תפעול / יציאה», and «יציאה» sits on top of «מסרונים», which makes those screens unreachable at a 900px height. Cause: `.sidebar nav` has `overflow:auto` but cannot shrink. Fix in styles.css: `.sidebar nav{flex:1 1 auto;min-height:0;overflow-y:auto}` `.side-user{flex:none;border-top:1px solid var(--line);background:#fff}`. Also tighten `.sidebar nav button` to height 40 and `.side-kicker` margins so everything fits in 900px without scrolling.
2. **Manager desktop, every page taller than 900px: the sidebar stops at 900px and the rest of the column is empty.** Seen in the full-page shots of board, calls, call-2401, parts, people, assets and reports. Fix: keep the sticky sidebar; use `.shell.manager{align-items:stretch}` plus a wrapper `.side-col{border-left:1px solid var(--side-line);background:#fff}` around the `<aside>` (grid item stretches, inner aside stays sticky 100vh).
3. **owner-call-2401 mobile: the fixed composer (65px) and the tabbar (56px) stack into 121px of fixed chrome that covers the «אישור / דחייה» quote buttons and the photos at the 844px viewport.** Fix: on the call screen, hide the tabbar (`.mode-customer.call ~ .tabbar{display:none}`, or don't render `<Tabbar>` on /c/calls/:id in Customer.jsx, since there's a «חזרה» link). Set the dock to `bottom:0` with `padding-bottom:calc(10px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom))`. Replace the stacked `.mode-customer.call` padding-bottom rules (lines 709, 1085, 1473, 1497, and 1567 with !important) with ONE rule: `padding-bottom:calc(88px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom))`.
4. **technician-call-2402 mobile: the 123px dock («תמונת סיום» + composer) covers the status segment and the «ממתין לחלק / ממתין ללקוח» buttons.** The overlap is 318×28px. Fix: move the «תמונת סיום» checkbox out of the dock into the «סטטוס בשטח» card (Employee.jsx), so the dock is a single 64px row. Then use `.mode-employee.call{padding-bottom:calc(80px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom))}` and delete the duplicate rules at lines 1442/1445/1497.
5. **technician-call-2402 desktop: the dock overlaps «מהלך הטיפול» and «הצעת מחיר» (overlap of 262×23px).** The 210px-wide call-side column is too narrow. Fix: `.mode-employee .call-grid{grid-template-columns:minmax(0,1fr) 320px}` at ≥1024, and render the composer inline at the end of the chat card on desktop (`@media(min-width:900px){.composer-dock{position:static;width:auto;box-shadow:none;border:0;padding:0}}`).
6. **manager-call-2401 desktop and mobile: the technician-only «תמונת סיום» checkbox shows in the manager's chat card.** Fix: in CallScreen.jsx, render it only when `role==='technician'`.
7. **owner-home mobile and desktop: the fixed tabbar sits over the «טיפול מונע» list.** It's what the full-page shot shows mid-page, and at the bottom the last list item scrolls under it with no clearance. Fix: `.shell.customer .page,.shell.employee .page{padding-bottom:calc(56px + 24px + env(safe-area-inset-bottom))}`. Also add `z-index:20; box-shadow:0 -1px 0 var(--line)` to the tabbar. Same for owner-assets, where the «משאבת בריכה» card and the «תקין» pill run under the tabbar.

## P1 — layout and consistency
8. **manager-calls mobile: each call card is about 205px and has 8 stacked lines, so the page is 10,868px for 45 calls.** Fix (Manager.jsx call card): row 1 shows `#2401` plus the status pill aligned to the end. Row 2 is the title (line-clamp 1). Row 3 is «נכס · חדר» in muted 13px. Row 4 holds the priority pill, overdue pill, technician and time on one line with gap 8. Target height ≤ 112px. Add pagination or «טען עוד» after 20 rows, on both desktop (3,072px table) and mobile.

10. **manager-call-2401 mobile: empty bands at y 1539–1916 and 1970–2107.** These are the two grey 150px photo placeholders, plus the gap before the timeline. Fix: when no photo exists, collapse to one 72px dashed «אין תמונות» row; otherwise use `.shots{grid-template-columns:repeat(3,1fr);aspect-ratio:1}`. Same for the desktop and owner/technician call screens.
11. **manager-buildings, desktop and mobile: a duplicate h3 «ציוד» inside every property card** (3× on the page; the sidebar also has «ציוד»). Fix: drop the h3 and render the equipment as a muted list headed by a small label `<span class="kicker">ציוד · 6</span>` (13px #5b6578). Equipment line text is currently 12px; raise it to 13px.
12. **manager-buildings desktop: the 3 cards have unequal content heights, and the «נכס חדש» form card leaves a 460px empty band below.** Fix: `.prop-grid{align-items:stretch}`, `.prop-card{display:flex;flex-direction:column}`, footer pill `margin-top:auto`. 14. **manager-people and manager-buildings mobile: an empty ~125px band before the last section** (1328–1453 and 1328–1428). Fix: section gap at most 24px (`.page>section+section{margin-top:24px}`); remove the stray `min-height` on the form card.
15. **owner-calls, owner-new, owner-alerts and technician-home/alerts, desktop and mobile: a 260–600px white void above the tabbar on short lists**, so the screen looks unfinished. On desktop, center the phone column (`max-width:560px;margin-inline:auto`) on the #f7f8fa bg with a 1px #e5e7eb frame, so it doesn't read as a broken page.
16. **Owner and technician desktop: the tabbar is 560px wide but pinned with `inset-inline:0`, so it isn't aligned with the content column.** Fix: `.shell.customer .tabbar,.shell.employee .tabbar{left:50%;right:auto;transform:translateX(-50%);width:min(560px,100%);border-radius:20px 20px 0 0}`.
17. **Card radius drift:** the measurement found `border-radius:0` on 6 `[class*=card]` elements on owner-home (`.home-line` list rows) and on the call-screen cards (`.call-card`). Fix: either all are cards (r20 plus soft shadow) or they're rows inside one r20 card; don't mix. For owner-home «טיפול מונע», put the 4 rows inside ONE white r20 card with 1px #e5e7eb dividers.
18. **Type scale drift:** sizes in use are 11.67px (26 nodes), 16.38px (6), and 12px (813 nodes). Locked scale: 13/14/16/22/28. Fix: `rg "11.6|16.38|font-size: ?12px" client/src/styles.css`, then map 11.67 and 12 to 13 and 16.38 to 16. Fallback fonts «Lucida Console» and «Helvetica Neue» are rendered on some nodes: set `font-family:inherit` on `code,kbd,.mono,input,select,textarea,button`.
19. **h1 drift:** the manager desktop h1 is 28px at y24, the manager mobile h1 is 24px, and the call screens use 22px at y102/150. Owner and technician screens use 22px at y72, except alerts, which sits at y81 because it has an extra wrapper. Fix: one `.page-title` of 22px mobile / 28px desktop, at the same top offset on every screen; remove the extra top padding on the alerts page.
20. **manager-board desktop: the charts card has the donut legend in 3 ragged rows, and bar labels sit far from the bars.** Fix: legend `display:grid;grid-template-columns:repeat(3,auto);gap:8px 16px`. Set the bars chart to `height:180px` with labels 8px under the bars. Make the 8 KPI tiles equal height (they are; keep it).
21. **manager-board desktop: the status column in the table shows 2–3 pills per row** (e.g., «שויכה · באיחור», «ממתינה לחלק · מושהה»), so it's crowded and ragged. Fix: show the status pill only, and mark overdue with a red 8px dot plus the «באיחור» text in the «נפתחה» column.
22. **manager-call-2401 desktop: the right detail card lists 6 label/value pairs stacked vertically, wasting 200px.** Fix: `dl{display:grid;grid-template-columns:repeat(3,1fr);gap:16px}`. Also make «ניווט / התקשרו» one 44px row (already); put the photos and timeline in separate cards.
23. **manager-map, desktop and mobile: Leaflet tiles overlap the sidebar and header when scrolled/mapped** (the measurement saw tiles under the wordmark, nav and h1). On mobile, the tiles extend past the viewport (WIDE). Fix: `.map-wrap{position:relative;overflow:hidden;border-radius:20px;isolation:isolate}` `.leaflet-container{z-index:0}`, and give the header/sidebar `z-index:10`.
24. **Duplicate «צלם» label**: `span.sr` is a 1px sr-only span holding 24px of text, so it's fine for screen readers but clips. Keep it; no change. (Listed only so it isn't "fixed" wrongly.)
